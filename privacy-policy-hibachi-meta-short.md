# Privacy Policy — Hibachi Five Star 30A (Short for Meta)

Effective date: August 13, 2026

This short privacy statement explains how Hibachi Five Star 30A ("we", "us") collects and uses personal data for advertising on Meta platforms.

Data collected: name and contact details you provide (e.g. to request a booking), device and browser information, IP address, site interactions, and identifiers from cookies and pixels.

How we use data: to respond to booking inquiries, operate and improve our site, and deliver and measure advertising on Meta (Facebook & Instagram).

Advertising tools: we use Meta Pixel and/or Conversion API to send hashed identifiers and event data (e.g. page views, booking conversions) to Meta to optimize ad delivery. Meta processes data under its policies.

Choices: you can opt out of interest-based ads via your Meta ad settings and disable cookies in your browser. For industry opt-out tools, visit https://www.aboutads.info/choices.
