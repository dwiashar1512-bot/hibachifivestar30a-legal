Advertising, Meta Pixel, and Conversion API

These tools may collect information about your activity on our site (e.g., pages visited, actions taken) and associate it with Meta accounts to deliver more relevant advertising.

Information sent to Meta may include device identifiers, IP address, hashed email addresses, and conversion data. Meta processes this data according to its own privacy policies. For more information and ad settings, visit Meta's privacy center: https://www.facebook.com/policy.php.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

We use cookies to remember preferences, enhance user experience, and collect analytics and advertising data. You can refuse cookies via your browser settings, but some site features may not work properly if cookies are disabled.

Sharing Data with Third Parties

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with third-party service providers who assist with site operations, payment processing, analytics, and advertising (including Meta). These providers have access only to what is necessary to perform their services and are required to keep data confidential.

Legal Bases for Processing (if EU/EEA applicable)

Where required by law, we process data based on your consent, the necessity to perform a contract (e.g., to complete a booking), or legal obligations.

Your Rights

- Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate data.
- Deletion: Request deletion of personal data (subject to legal limits).

To exercise these rights, contact us at fivestarshibachi@gmail.com.

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal data. Data is retained only as long as necessary for the purposes described or as required by law.
